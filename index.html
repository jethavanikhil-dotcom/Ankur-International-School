Welcome to the Ankur International School New Maninagar

Our Value System

At Ankur International School, we believe that every child is born with unique talents, limitless imagination, and a natural curiosity to understand the world. Our goal is to create an environment where learning feels joyful, meaningful, and full of discovery.

We combine modern teaching methods with a caring approach, helping children explore new ideas, express themselves creatively, and build a strong academic foundation.

With dedicated teachers, interactive classrooms, and activity-based learning, we ensure every child grows confidently, develops essential life skills, and becomes ready for the challenges of tomorrow. At Ankur International School, we don’t just educate children—we nurture happy, confident, and responsible individuals.

Something to try together: trace the two constellations below and they will tell you what we are aiming for.

Our Vision

To be a school where every child discovers what they are curious about, and grows into a confident, kind and capable young person who is ready for the world ahead.

Our Mission

To nurture curiosity through joyful, activity-based learning, to build strong values and life skills alongside academic excellence, and to work with every family in raising happy, responsible children.
